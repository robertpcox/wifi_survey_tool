The dominant failure is Cisco Spaces serving stale position fixes: the underlying blue-dot location often doesn't change for many seconds at a time, so the dot sits still while the person keeps walking. Network round-trip time — a separate, much smaller factor — stays under a second most of the time.

Methodology

  • Stuck = consecutive samples where the reported position moved <1 m while ground truth moved >5 m over the same interval.
  • Error = haversine distance between the reported fix and the ground-truth track, linearly interpolated between the 144 walk check-ins.
  • Staleness = client receive time minus the Cisco Spaces lastSeen timestamp on the fix.
  • No HTTP non-200 responses occurred during the route capture.
